Wealth Tax Simulator

Estimate annual wealth taxes for different regions. Pick a model (Zurich / Mallorca), enter total personal wealth, and see the estimated annual tax (absolute and as % of wealth). Models are implemented from official sources — sources are linked in the repository.

About the models

The site includes two region-specific example models implemented from official sources (see SOURCES.md). Mallorca (Balearic Islands) implements the 2024 autonomous-region exemption and bracket scale. Zurich implements the cantonal progressive tariff and applies the 2024 Staatssteuerfuss and Stadt Zürich Gemeindesteuerfuss multipliers to compute the total local tax.

These models are for illustrative calculation and were implemented from official canton/region documentation. Verify with official calculators or tax advisers for legal/financial decisions.
